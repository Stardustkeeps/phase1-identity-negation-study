## 4. WHY THE BRIDGE IS WEAKER THAN IT LOOKS — against my own interest

**It is not an independent replication.** Same ten templates, same runner, same pinned substrate,
same corpus. It adds **repetitions**, not stimulus breadth. It reduces within-template noise; it
cannot tell us the effect generalises beyond these ten injections. **Anyone citing it as
independent confirmation is overclaiming, including me.**

**Pooling is legitimate but must be declared in advance**, and I am declaring it: if the direction
holds, the honest statement is *"52 vs 52 across two collections on one substrate with one
stimulus set,"* **not** *"replicated."*
